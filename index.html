The Director's Cut

Kavishanke Adithya is a Sri Lankan based filmmaker and the founder of Kalu Creatives, known for his creative approach with a passion for cinematic visuals and storytelling.

He is a passionate videographer and visual storyteller with a strong interest in creating cinematic and meaningful visual experiences. With a keen eye for composition, lighting, movement, and detail, he approaches every project with creativity and a commitment to capturing stories in an authentic and engaging way.

He is currently an Undergraduate in BA (Hons) in Creative Multimedia at NIBM, building his expertise across various areas of digital media and creative production. He has also successfully completed the Advanced Diploma in Advertising and Multimedia at NIBM, the Certificate Course on Filming at the Asian Film Academy, and the Certificate Course in Television Cinematography and Lighting at Sri Lanka Rupavahini Corporation. His academic and professional training in multimedia, advertising, filmmaking, cinematography, and lighting has helped him develop a well-rounded understanding of visual production.

Combining his technical knowledge with a creative approach, Kavishanke continues to explore new ways of visual storytelling through videography, filmmaking, and digital media. His goal is to create visuals that not only look compelling but also communicate emotions, ideas, and stories effectively.
